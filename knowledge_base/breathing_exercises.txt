# Evidence-Based Calming Breathing Techniques

### 1. Box Breathing (Square Breathing)
Used by professionals and therapists to quickly regulate the autonomic nervous system when heart rate elevates:
- **Inhale**: Breathe in slowly through your nose for a silent count of 4.
- **Hold**: Hold the breath gently without straining for a count of 4.
- **Exhale**: Release the breath smoothly through your mouth or nose for a count of 4.
- **Hold Empty**: Rest with empty lungs gently for a count of 4.
Repeat this cycle 4 to 6 times. Feel your heart rate stabilize.

### 2. The Physiological Sigh
A rapid, neurobiologically proven way to lower autonomic arousal in seconds:
- Take a deep, gentle inhale through the nose.
- Right at the top of that breath, take a second, sharp sip of air to fully expand the alveoli in your lungs.
- Release a long, slow, audible exhale through your mouth until your lungs feel relaxed and empty.
Repeat just 2 to 3 times to immediately trigger the parasympathetic "rest and digest" response.

### 3. 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath
Ideal for settling racing thoughts before bedtime or during overwhelming worry:
- Inhale quietly through your nose for 4 seconds.
- Hold your breath comfortably for 7 seconds.
- Exhale completely through your mouth with a soft whoosh sound for 8 seconds.
Repeat for 4 cycles.
