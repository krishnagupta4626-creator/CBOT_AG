# Sensory Grounding: The 5-4-3-2-1 Technique

The 5-4-3-2-1 technique is a simple, highly effective sensory grounding exercise designed to calm the nervous system during moments of acute anxiety, panic, or sensory overwhelm.

### How to Practice
Take a gentle, unhurried breath in through your nose, letting your belly expand, and exhale softly. Notice your immediate surroundings and quietly name:
1. **5 things you can see**: Look for subtle details—a pattern of shadows, a texture on a wall, a reflection on a screen, the color of your pen.
2. **4 things you can feel**: The texture of your sweater against your wrists, the firmness of the floor beneath your feet, the cool air touching your skin, or the smooth back of your phone.
3. **3 things you can hear**: Distant traffic, the hum of a fan or computer, the wind against the window, or the quiet sound of your own breathing.
4. **2 things you can smell**: A cup of tea, laundry detergent on your clothing, the scent of rain, or simply the neutral air in the room.
5. **1 thing you can taste**: A lingering sip of water, mint, or simply pausing to swallow and notice the sensation in your mouth.

Finish with one slow, deep belly breath. Remind yourself: "I am safe in this present moment. This feeling is temporary, and it will pass."
